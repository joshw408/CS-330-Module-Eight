# CS-330-Module-Eight
Module Eight Journal

**How do I approach designing software?**

**What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?**

Designing my scene helped me learn how to plan object placement and scale so everything felt balanced and intentional, especially with the shelves and Game Boy cartridges. I also developed a better eye for how lighting and textures can be used to highlight important objects without making the scene feel cluttered.

**What design process did you follow for your project work?**

I started by blocking out the scene using simple shapes to establish the shelves and overall layout before adding detail. Once the structure felt right, I focused on refining textures, materials, and lighting to give the cartridges more visual depth and realism.

**How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?**

Building the scene in layers helped me avoid unnecessary rework and made it easier to adjust individual elements like spacing or rotation. This approach will be useful in future projects where visual clarity and organization matter just as much as functionality.

**How do I approach developing programs?**

**What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?**

I relied on reusable functions for transformations, materials, and textures so I could apply consistent behavior across multiple objects like the cartridge stands. This made my code easier to manage and helped keep the scene visually consistent.

**How did iteration factor into your development?**

Iteration was a major part of my process, especially when fine tuning object positions and rotations so the cartridges sat naturally on the stands. Small changes followed by visual testing helped me gradually improve the realism and alignment of the scene.

**How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?**

As the milestones progressed, I became more comfortable making intentional changes instead of experimenting randomly. I learned to think ahead about how each change would affect the rest of the scene, which made the final assembly feel more controlled and polished.

**How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?**

**How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?**

Working on this scene helped me better understand transformations, coordinate systems, and how math directly affects what appears on screen. Seeing these concepts applied visually made them easier to grasp and will help in future coursework that builds on graphics or simulation concepts.

**How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?**

This project gave me hands on experience designing and refining a complete visual system, not just writing isolated pieces of code. The ability to think visually, debug graphical issues, and iterate toward a polished result is a skill I can carry into many software development roles.
